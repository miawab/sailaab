Judges will ask: how do you know you picked the right houses? So we ran a blind test. We scored all 44,000 houses using only their height above the river and their distance to it, without telling the score whether they flooded in 2022. Then we checked against the UN's satellite map of what really flooded. In Nowshera, 76 percent of the houses we flag really flooded, against 46 percent of the rest. In Dadu it's 62 against 39. So the houses we flag flooded about 1.6 times as often. It's not perfect, and we say so: free height data is rough and the UN map has 375 m pixels, so we rank houses by risk rather than promise exact water depths.

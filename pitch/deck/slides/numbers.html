In the summer of 2022, floods affected 33 million people in Pakistan. 1,739 people died. About two million homes were damaged or destroyed, and damage and losses passed 30 billion dollars. These numbers are from the government's post-disaster needs assessment with the UN and World Bank, and from NDMA.

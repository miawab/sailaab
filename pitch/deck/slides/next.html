Source: World Bank, “Preparing better for monsoons: lessons from the 2025 flash floods in Pakistan”, Sep 2026

Where does this go next? One: WhatsApp and SMS alerts, working with the provincial disaster authorities. Two: mosque loudspeakers. After the 2025 floods, the World Bank found that one of the best last-mile warnings was a phone call to an imam, who warned the village on the loudspeaker. A short voice note is exactly what you'd play there. Three: Pakistan's own river gauges, to fix the size errors we found at Nowshera. Four: every district, because the data we use already covers the whole country.

What we do about it

Add Pakistan’s gauge readings where they exist. On big channels like Dadu’s, GloFAS already shows 2022 as the worst year on record

Federal Flood Commission daily flood reports, 1 Aug to 15 Sep 2022, 06:00 readings converted from cusecs to m³/s; GloFAS v4 via Open-Meteo. Gaps are days with no report

We didn't want to just trust our data source, so we checked it. Hasib read every Federal Flood Commission daily report for August and September 2022 and lined up the Kabul River at Nowshera with GloFAS, the free model we use. GloFAS got the timing right: the wave rises from the 26th and peaks around the 28th to 30th. But it saw only about a fifth of the real flow, because the Kabul flood came from very heavy local rain that a 5-kilometre global model smooths out. So the next step is clear: plug in Pakistan's own gauge readings where they exist. Where the flood came down a big channel, like Dadu, GloFAS clearly shows 2022 as the worst year on record.

The problem

Warnings named districts. Families needed to know about their own house

A district bulletin can’t tell one family whether their street floods on Thursday, or what to do tonight.

Warnings didn’t reliably reach remote communities, or people who don’t read the language they were written in.

Source: ReliefWeb (UN OCHA), review of disaster response in Pakistan, 2010 vs 2022

The warnings that existed were for whole districts. But water doesn't flood a district evenly: one street floods, the next one up the slope doesn't. And a UN review of 2022 found the warnings didn't reliably reach remote communities, or people who speak other languages. So the question we asked was: can we warn one family, about one house, in their own language?

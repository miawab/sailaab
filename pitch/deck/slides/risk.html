THE RISK SCORE

How we score a house

Risk score = river severity × house exposure × 100

River severity: the 7-day forecast against 38 years of history (1984–2021)

LevelWhen the forecast river flow is…

House exposure, 0 to 1

  • Height above the river

Judges will ask this, so here it is plainly. First, how bad is the river? We take the 7-day forecast and compare it with the same river's own history from 1984 to 2021. Above its normal 90 percent level is Watch, above 99 percent is Warning, above the 2022 peak is Danger. Second, how exposed is this house? A house that sits low, close to the river, and flooded in 2022 is very exposed. Multiply the two and you get a score from 0 to 100. [Check the exact exposure weights with Zarrar before the pitch.]

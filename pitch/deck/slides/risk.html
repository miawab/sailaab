The risk score

How we score a house

house score = river severity × exposure × 100

River severity

This week’s forecast against the river’s own history (1997 to 2021)

  • Safebelow its 90th percentile
  • Watch90th to 99th percentile
  • Warningabove its 99th percentile
  • Danger90% of its 2022 peak or more

House exposure, 0 to 1

From the house’s own data

  • Height above the river50%
  • Distance to the river30%
  • Flooded in 202220%

Score 25+ watch · 50+ warning · 75+ danger

Judges will ask this, so here it is plainly. First, how bad is the river? We take this week's forecast and compare it with the same river's own history from 1997 to 2021. Above its normal 90 percent level is Watch, above 99 percent is Warning, and 90 percent of the 2022 peak or more is Danger. Second, how exposed is this house? Half of it is how low the house sits above the river, 30 percent how close it is, and 20 percent whether it flooded in 2022. Multiply the two and you get a score from 0 to 100: 25 and up is watch, 50 warning, 75 danger. So when the river rises, the low house by the bank turns red first. Honest limits: the 50, 30, 20 weights are our choice, checked against 2022, not fitted on many floods; and GloFAS covers major rivers only, not flash floods or city drains.

HOW IT WORKS

Ground height

Risk engine

3D map

Voice alert

Under 20 seconds, in 4 languages

All sources are free and open. No paid APIs. The full list is in the project README.

Everything we use is free, global data. The river forecast comes from GloFAS, the global flood forecasting system, through Open-Meteo. The buildings are Google's Open Buildings. The 2022 flood is UNOSAT's satellite map, and the ground height comes from terrain tiles. Our risk engine joins them, and the result goes to the map and the voice alert. Because the data covers the whole country, the same pipeline works for any district.

How it works

Free global data in, one house’s warning out

Data

  • River forecast

    GloFAS v4 via Open-Meteo, 7 days ahead

  • Every building

    Google Open Buildings v3

  • The 2022 flood

    UNOSAT satellite flood map

  • Ground height

    Copernicus DEM, 30 m

  • Rivers, rain and hills

    OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo weather and terrain

Risk engine

Compares this week’s river forecast with the river’s own history, then with each house’s height, distance and 2022 flooding

Out

  • The map

    Rivers and houses coloured by level

  • The house card

    Level, reasons, 7-day chart

  • The voice note

    20 s or less, 4 languages

All free and open, no paid APIs and no keys. The full list with licences is in the project README

Everything we use is free, global data. The river forecast comes from GloFAS, the global flood forecasting system, through Open-Meteo. The buildings are Google's Open Buildings. The 2022 flood is UNOSAT's satellite map, and the ground height is the Copernicus 30-metre elevation model. Our risk engine joins them, and the result goes to the map, the house card and the voice note. Because the data covers the whole country, the same pipeline works for any district.

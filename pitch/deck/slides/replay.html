Replay · Dadu, Sindh · 2022

Near Johi, the water passed our warning line 10 days before the record peak

River flow from GloFAS v4 via Open-Meteo, Johi flood channel (26.78° N, 67.63° E). Our lines for this point: watch 12, warning 305, danger 14,833 m³/s (90% of the 2022 peak). The 27 Aug peak is the highest day in its record since 1997

This is the moment from our demo. West of Dadu, the flood channel past Johi is almost dry in a normal year. In August 2022 it crossed our warning line on the 17th, and ten days later, on the 27th, it carried 16,481 cubic metres a second, the highest day in its record. That matches what happened: Dadu's flood came from the west, hill torrents heading for Manchar Lake, not from the Indus. To be precise about what this shows: these are the river flows as they happened, replayed through our risk engine. Whether the forecast issued at the time would have shown it days ahead is our next test.

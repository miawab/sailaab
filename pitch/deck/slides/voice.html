4 levels × 4 languages · each under 20 seconds

Safety advice follows NDMA and PDMA flood guidance.

Many people in flood areas can't read a long bulletin, and English is nobody's first language there. So every alert is a voice note, under 20 seconds, in Urdu, Sindhi, Punjabi or English. It says three things: what's happening, what to do right now, and where to go. Then play the Urdu warning voice note here if there's time.

Safety advice follows NDMA and PDMA flood guidance

Many people in flood areas can't read a long bulletin, and English is nobody's first language there. So every alert is a voice note, 20 seconds or less, in Urdu, Sindhi, Punjabi or English. It says three things: what's happening, what to do right now, and where to go. Sixteen notes in all. The Sindhi ones are recorded by a person; Urdu and English use Microsoft's free neural voices; Punjabi uses Meta's free MMS voice. Play the Urdu warning note here if there's time.

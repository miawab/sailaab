THE SOLUTION

Sailaab warns one family about one house

01

See it

A 3D map of Pakistan. Every major river glows in its 7-day forecast colour, and in the showcase districts every house stands in 3D.

02

Understand it

Your house gets Safe, Watch, Warning or Danger, with the reasons: how high it sits above the river, how far away it is, and whether it flooded in 2022.

03

Hear it

A voice note under 20 seconds in Urdu, Sindhi, Punjabi or English: what is happening, what to do now, where to go.

Sailaab does three things. You see it: a 3D map of Pakistan, rivers coloured by their forecast, and in our showcase districts, every single house. You understand it: your house gets one of four levels, and we tell you why. And you hear it: a short voice note in your language, like a WhatsApp voice note from a relative, telling you what to do.

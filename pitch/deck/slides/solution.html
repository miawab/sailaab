What Sailaab does

One family, one house, one warning they can hear

See it

A map of Pakistan with 235 points on 20 rivers, coloured by this week’s forecast. In two districts, Nowshera and Dadu, 44,000 real houses.

Understand it

Each house gets one of four levels, with its reasons: height above the river, distance to it, and whether it flooded in 2022.

SafeWatchWarningDanger

Hear it

A voice note of 20 seconds or less in Urdu, Sindhi, Punjabi or English: what is happening, what to do now, where to go.

Sailaab does three things. You see it: a map of all of Pakistan, rivers coloured by this week's forecast, and in our two showcase districts, every single house. You understand it: your house gets one of four levels, safe, watch, warning or danger, and we tell you why. And you hear it: a short voice note in your language, like a WhatsApp voice note from a relative, telling you what to do.
